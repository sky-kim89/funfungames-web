Account and Data Deletion Request

FunFunGames (뻔뻔게임즈) · Effective date: November 24, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account and associated data for the following games published on Google Play by FunFunGames (뻔뻔게임즈).

  • Pixel General (픽셀 제너럴)
  • Stop the Heroes: Demon Lord Defense (용사를 막아라: 마왕성 디펜스)

Our games do not require a separate sign-up; they use your Google Play Games account (Google sign-in). We do not create our own accounts, and you can delete the data linked to our games using the methods below.

1. Request deletion by email

Send an email to support@funfungamesstudio.com with the following information. Using the subject line [Account Deletion Request] Game name helps us process it faster.

  1. The name of the game
  2. Your Google Play Games profile name (Gamer ID) linked to the game
  3. What you want deleted: your account and all data, or only some data (see Section 3)

After verifying your identity, we will delete the data within 10 days of receiving your request and notify you by email. We may ask for additional information to verify your identity.

2. Delete it yourself

Google Play Games data

Cloud saves (saved games) and achievements are stored in your Google Play Games account. In the Google Play Games app, open the account and data deletion menu in your profile settings, choose the game, and delete that game's data yourself.

Data stored on your device

Game progress stored on your device can be removed by uninstalling the game, or via device Settings → Apps → the game → Storage → Clear data.

3. Data that is deleted or retained

DataWhat happens
Cloud save data (game progress, owned currency and items)Deleted
AchievementsDeleted
Customer inquiry records (email address, inquiry content)Deleted
Gameplay analytics (device identifiers, gameplay records, etc.)Not linked to your account; anonymized or deleted 12 months after collection
Purchase records (if you made purchases)Kept for 5 years as required by Korea's Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, then deleted

If you want to delete only some data without deleting your account (for example, only cloud save data or only inquiry records), list the items in the email described in Section 1.

Deleted data and purchased items cannot be recovered. For more details, see our Privacy Policy.
