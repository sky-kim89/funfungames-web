Account and Data Deletion Request

FunFunGames (뻔뻔게임즈) · Effective date: November 24, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account and associated data for the following games published on Google Play by FunFunGames (뻔뻔게임즈).

  • Pixel General (픽셀 제너럴)
  • Stop the Heroes: Demon Lord Defense (용사를 막아라: 마왕성 디펜스)

Our games do not require a separate sign-up; they use your Google Play Games account (Google sign-in). We do not create our own accounts, and you can delete the data linked to our games yourself using the methods below.

1. Data that is deleted or retained

DataWhat happens
Cloud save data (game progress, owned currency and items)Deleted
AchievementsDeleted
Game data stored on your deviceDeleted
Customer inquiry records (email address, inquiry content)Deleted on request (see Section 3)
Gameplay analytics (device identifiers, gameplay records, etc.)Not linked to your account; anonymized or deleted 12 months after collection
Purchase records (if you made purchases)Kept for 5 years as required by Korea's Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, then deleted

Deleted data and purchased items cannot be recovered. For more details, see our Privacy Policy.

2. How to delete

Google Play Games data (cloud saves and achievements)

Cloud saves (saved games) and achievements are stored in your Google Play Games account. In the Google Play Games app, open the account and data deletion menu in your profile settings, choose the game, and delete that game's data.

Data stored on your device

Game progress stored on your device can be removed by uninstalling the game, or via device Settings → Apps → the game → Storage → Clear data.

3. Further inquiries

If the methods above do not work, or you have other requests such as deleting your customer inquiry records, email support@funfungamesstudio.com with the game name and your request. We will handle it within 10 days of receiving it and let you know the result.
