Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

This is an English translation of the Korean privacy policy (개인정보처리방침). If the two versions differ, the Korean version prevails.

FunFunGames (뻔뻔게임즈) (the "Company") complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and other applicable laws. We have established and published this Privacy Policy to protect users' personal information and to handle related concerns promptly.

Our games can be played without signing up or logging in, and we do not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, phone number or address. Your game progress is stored on your device.

2. Information we process and why

We process only the minimum information needed for the purposes below. If a purpose changes, we will take the necessary steps, such as obtaining your prior consent.

CategoryInformationPurpose
Gameplay analytics Device identifiers (a random ID generated by GameAnalytics, and the advertising ID if permitted on your device), IP address, device model and OS version, country and language settings, game version, session date/time and session information, gameplay records (stage progress, features used, etc.), error logs Game balancing, bug analysis and fixes, usage statistics

Game progress (save data) is stored only on your device and is not sent to the Company. Gameplay analytics data does not include your name or contact details, so the Company cannot link it to a specific individual.

3. Retention period

  • Gameplay analytics: Under the retention policy of our processor (GameAnalytics), device identifiers, IP addresses and similar data are anonymized or deleted 12 months after collection. Statistical data that cannot identify individuals may be used without time limits.
  • Customer inquiries: Kept for 3 years after the inquiry is resolved, then destroyed.

4. Provision to third parties

We process your personal information only within the purposes described in Section 2. We provide it to third parties only in cases permitted under Articles 17 and 18 of the Personal Information Protection Act, such as with your consent or where specifically required by law. We currently do not provide personal information to any third party.

7. Automatic collection and how to opt out

We use an analytics tool (the GameAnalytics SDK) that relies on device identifiers to analyze gameplay. You can refuse or limit this collection as follows:

  • Android advertising ID: Device Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads → "Delete advertising ID"
  • Turn off the network: If you play with your device's network connection turned off, no analytics data is sent.

Our official website does not use cookies.

8. Destruction of personal information

We destroy personal information without delay once its retention period has expired or its purpose has been achieved.

  • Procedure: We identify the information to be destroyed and destroy it with the approval of the privacy officer.
  • Method: Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered, and paper documents are shredded or incinerated.
  • Game data stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game.

9. Your rights and how to exercise them

You may at any time ask the Company to let you access, correct or delete your personal information, to suspend its processing, or to withdraw your consent.

  • How to request: Email your request to support@funfungamesstudio.com. We will act on it and notify you of the result within 10 days of receiving it.
  • Gameplay analytics: This data is collected only with device identifiers, without names or contact details. If the Company cannot verify which data belongs to you, access or deletion may not be possible. In that case you can stop further collection using the methods in Section 7, and data already collected will be anonymized or deleted after the period in Section 3.
  • You may also exercise your rights through a representative, such as a legal guardian or a person you have authorized.
  • You cannot request deletion of information that other laws require us to keep, and requests to suspend processing may be limited under Article 37(2) of the Personal Information Protection Act.

10. Children under 14

We do not offer features that require a legal guardian's consent to process the personal information of children under 14 (for example, account sign-up or personalized ads). If we introduce such features in the future, we will set up a process to obtain the guardian's consent and explain it in a way children can easily understand.

If we learn that a child under 14 has sent us personal information, for example through a customer inquiry, and we cannot confirm a guardian's consent, we will destroy that information without delay.

11. Security measures

  • Administrative: We keep the number of people who handle personal information to a minimum and check our processor's level of data protection.
  • Technical: We apply access controls such as two-factor authentication to accounts that handle personal information, and encrypt data in transit (HTTPS).
  • Physical: We keep devices and documents containing personal information in a lockable place.
