PROJECT 001

Pixel General

픽셀 제너럴

Build your army. Make your choices. Rise again.

Pixel General is a tactical auto-battler roguelite where generals and their soldiers fight together. Pick a Knight, Archer, Mage or Shield general to start your journey with a small squad, then recruit more generals and grow your forces stage by stage until you command a massive army.

Battles play out automatically, but the decisive moments are yours. Aim skills straight at clustered enemies, and shape each run with your mix of abilities, traits and equipment. Fall in battle and your rebirth points and relics carry over, so your next campaign starts stronger.

◆Game Loop

  1. FormChoose and position your generals
  2. FightPush through 30 stages of auto-battles
  3. GrowGain traits and abilities from shops and events
  4. RebirthUnlock relics on defeat to power up your next run

◆Key Features

From a small squad to a vast army

The more generals you gather and the more troops you raise, the fuller the battlefield gets. Each general leads dozens of soldiers, building up to battles with hundreds of units.

Aim the decisive blow yourself

The AI casts skills on its own, but you can tap a general's card at any moment and aim the skill right where the enemies are packed.

Choices that define your army

Three-way ability picks, class traits and class-combo synergies. Every run's choices build a different army.

Defeat is not the end

After each run, spend rebirth points to grow your relic tree. Relic effects are permanent and become the starting point of your next journey.
