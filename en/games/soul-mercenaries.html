Soul-Absorbing Mercenaries

Working Title · 영혼을 흡수하는 용병단

The souls you give them decide how they fight.

Soul-Absorbing Mercenaries is a 2D RPG about running a mercenary company and exploring dungeons. When a mercenary absorbs a soul taken from a monster, they gain new stats, behavior patterns, and active and passive skills. Two swordsmen can play completely different roles depending on the souls they carry.

Hire mercenaries, form a party and send them on expeditions. Your mercenaries explore and fight on their own, and you can give movement orders whenever you need to. Invest the resources you bring back into village facilities such as the guild, blacksmith, training ground and library to prepare for the next expedition.

◆Key Features

Mercenaries shaped by souls

Have mercenaries absorb monster souls to gain new abilities, and preserve the souls you want to save for later. Soul slots increase as mercenaries level up.

Behavior patterns are tactics

Attack, defense, movement and encounter patterns define how each mercenary fights. Combine slashes, blocks, flanking and taunts to split roles across your party.

An expedition that moves on its own

Your party explores fog-covered dungeons by itself and defeats the guardian to open the exit. Give direct movement orders whenever you want.

A village that backs your expeditions

Upgrade village facilities like the guild, blacksmith, training ground, library and soul altar as you prepare to delve deeper.
