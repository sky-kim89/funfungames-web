PROJECT 004

Stop the Heroes

Demon Lord Defense · 용사를 막아라: 마왕성 디펜스

This time, you're on the monsters' side.

Stop the Heroes is a card-summoning defense roguelite where you play the summoner guarding the Demon Lord's castle against waves of heroes. It flips Pixel General's battles around: heroes armed with classes, skills and gear are now the invaders, and you command the monster army.

Read your hand and decide what to summon. Drag a card onto one of five lanes and the monster joins the fight, or play a skill card to break the heroes' advance. Grow your deck with rewards, upgrade and evolve your cards, and even if you fall, relics prepare you for the next attempt.

◆Key Features

Drag cards, summon your army

Drop a monster card from your hand onto any lane and it joins the battle instantly. From slimes to liches, your summoned monsters fight together.

Break their advance with skill cards

When monsters alone aren't enough, play a skill card. One card dropped on a crowd of enemies can turn the tide.

Upgrade and evolve

Add new power to your cards, and upgraded cards evolve. Face stronger heroes with evolved monsters.

Stop the boss heroes

Boss stages bring powerful boss heroes to your gates. It's the moment your deck is put to the test.
