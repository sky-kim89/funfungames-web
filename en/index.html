About Us

Small team, big battlefields

FunFunGames (뻔뻔게임즈) is an indie game studio developing original, immersive games for mobile and PC. Our goal is to bring fun to players around the world through Google Play and Steam.

We build with Unity and put a lot of care into running screen-filling battles smoothly. In every game, progress carries forward even when a run ends.

01

Units that fight on their own

From army clashes with hundreds of soldiers to small-party dungeon dives, units make their own decisions in battle.

02

Builds made by choices

Combine skills, gear, traits, cards and souls to craft your own strategy.

03

Progress that never stops

Even when you fall, your growth remains. Relics and village upgrades make every new attempt stronger.
